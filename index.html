About this Website

I intially created this website for a class project on where I can live freely with no permit in Canada. I've since changed it to photo booths which is more managable and, honestly, something I care about more often in my daily life. Plus, it's just fun!

The data is comprised of places I know exist and more locations will be added as I find them throughout the city. I am working on adding a layer for temporary installations. If you know of a location with a permanent or temporary booth that you would like to add, email the details to: sydneyreymond@gmail.com
